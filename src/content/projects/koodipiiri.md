This site was created to address the lack of Finnish beginner programming courses focusing on microcontrollers. The goal is to
make microcontroller programming easily accessible for students and provide them with a starting point for learning and building
projects.

The course consists of written instructions and exercises, which are conducted using the online simulator Wokwi. This approach
minimizes friction for beginners, as no purchases, downloads, or even sign-ups are required.

You too can start learning the basics of microcontroller programming at [koodipiiri.fi](https://koodipiiri.fi).

Developed in collaboration with Tekniikan akateemiset TEK.
